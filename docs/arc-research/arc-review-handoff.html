03 · REDUCE CLUTTER WITHOUT DISCARDING WORK

Tidy, Sleep and Archive are three different things.

One changes organization, one releases resources, and one removes an eligible tab after saving recovery.

Separate versions
Why we did it

A cleaner sidebar is not worth losing an upload, a playing page or an unfinished form.

What we gain

More reversible actions and clearer protection of active work. We have not claimed measured memory savings.

Tidy tabs, with a way backWritten separately
The problem
Automatic grouping is risky if it cannot be undone safely.
What changed
Keep local grouping, and add one-step undo that restores the eligible order and sections without reopening pages.
What you gain
You can try tidying without sacrificing tabs opened or edited afterwards.

Next: Group tabs, open a new one, then undo. Also change or close an old tab before undoing.
